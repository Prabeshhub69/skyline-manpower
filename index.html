Our Story

Born from the
mountains,
built for the world.

It started with a single question: why do the most hardworking people in South Asia have to navigate a broken, exploitative system just to find honest work abroad?

Skyline was founded in Kathmandu — not as a staffing firm, but as a bridge. A bridge built on the belief that every Nepali worker deserves a safe passage, a fair wage, and a future they can be proud of. From the tea hills of Ilam to the steel sites of Doha, we carry that mission with every deployment.

"Every passport we process is someone's dream departing Tribhuvan. We take that seriously."

— Founding Team, Skyline
