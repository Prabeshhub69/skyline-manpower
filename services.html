Overseas Manpower Services

End-to-end recruitment solutions from Nepal to Qatar, Malaysia, UAE, and KSA.

Sourcing & Screening

Overseas Recruitment

We supply skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled Nepali professionals across diverse industries such as Construction, Hospitality, Oil & Gas, Security, and Manufacturing.

Our expansive database and robust sourcing network reaching remote areas of Nepal ensure we find hardworking candidates that match your specific demands.

  • Bulk Hiring Campaigns
  • Advertising & Outreach
  • Initial Screening
  • Candidate Shortlisting
Documentation

Visa & Processing Assistance

Navigating the complex bureaucratic processes in Nepal is our specialty. We handle obtaining approvals from the Department of Foreign Employment (DOFE).

We ensure all document attestations, visa stamping, and labor approvals are processed in record time to meet your deployment schedule.

  • Embassy Attestations
  • DOFE Labor Clearances
  • Contract Signing
  • Police Clearances
Preparation

Medicals & Trade Testing

Quality and health are paramount. All short-listed candidates undergo strict medical examinations at GAMCA or other government-approved diagnostic centers.

For skilled categories (welders, carpenters, drivers, etc.), we facilitate rigorous practical trade tests in affiliated certified technical institutes before final selection.

  • Pre-Employment Medicals
  • Institute Trade Testing
  • Pre-Departure Orientation
  • Cultural Briefing
Mobilization

Deployment & Logistical Support

Our commitment doesn't end at selection. We manage the entire mobilization process to ensure workers arrive at your site on schedule and ready to work.

From flight bookings and airport assistance in Kathmandu to coordinating group departures and overseas reception, we handle all the logistical details.

  • Flight & Travel Arrangements
  • Airport Assistance & Drop-off
  • Group Mobilization
  • Overseas Onboarding Support

Our Manpower Process

A streamlined, 10-step recruitment journey ensuring quality, compliance, and on-time deployment.

Initiation

Demand Letter from Foreign Employer

The overseas employer issues an official demand letter specifying job categories, quantities, salaries, and deployment terms.

1
2
Compliance

Government Pre-Approval

Skyline submits the demand to DOFE (Dept. of Foreign Employment) and obtains official pre-approval before any recruitment begins.

Outreach

Job Advertisement

Vacancy campaigns are published via national newspapers, digital platforms, radio, and our extensive rural outreach network across Nepal.

3
4
Selection

Application & Screening

Candidates apply and our team shortlists based on qualifications, prior experience, and suitability against the employer's requirements.

Assessment

Interview & Trade Test

An employer panel or appointed proxy conducts interviews. Skilled roles (welders, drivers, carpenters) undergo live practical trade testing at certified institutes.

5
6
Health

Medical Check-Up

All selected candidates undergo pre-employment medical examinations at GAMCA-approved or other government-certified diagnostic centers.

Documentation

Visa Processing

Skyline coordinates embassy stamping, contract attestation, police clearances, and work visa issuance for every selected candidate.

7
8
Preparation

Orientation Training

A mandatory pre-departure session covers destination country culture, worker rights and duties, workplace safety, and emergency procedures.

Finalisation

Final Approval & Documentation

DOFE labour clearance, insurance certificates, and departure permits are issued and cross-verified before travel arrangements are confirmed.

9
10
Deployment

Departure & Deployment

Candidates depart for their destination country — fully documented, medically cleared, and prepared for a successful overseas placement.
