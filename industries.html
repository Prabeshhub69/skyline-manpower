Nepali workers are renowned for their naturally hospitable and polite demeanor, making them a perfect fit for the service-oriented hospitality sector. With strong English communication skills and an innate ability to adapt to diverse cultural environments, they bring a high level of dedication and an excellent work ethic to every role they undertake.
