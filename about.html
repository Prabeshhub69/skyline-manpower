About Skyline

Nepal's trusted partner in ethical foreign employment and overseas recruitment.

Government Approved

Uplifting Lives by Connecting Nepali Talent Globally

Skyline is a fully licensed and registered overseas manpower agency headquartered in Kathmandu, Nepal. Our mission is to provide life-changing international career opportunities to Nepali citizens while fulfilling the massive labor demands of rapidly growing economies in the Middle East and Southeast Asia.

We pride ourselves on our ethical recruitment practices, zero-tolerance policy against worker exploitation, and strict adherence to the labor laws of both Nepal and the host countries. When you partner with us, you are guaranteed a fully compliant, medically fit, and highly motivated workforce.
